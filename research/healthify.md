# Healthify (formerly HealthifyMe): research brief

*Compiled 2026-09-27 from web search results. Most primary news pages could not be fetched from this environment, so the figures below are cited as they appeared in search snippets. Treat them as indicative, not audited.*

> **Name note:** There is also an unrelated US "Healthify" (social-care referral software) that has its own PitchBook/Tracxn profile. This brief covers the Bengaluru-based AI nutrition and weight-loss company.

## Product
- **Ria:** an AI nutrition coach launched in 2018. It gained "Snap" photo-based food logging in the Dec 2023 redesign and real-time voice conversation in Dec 2025 (TechCrunch).
- **Three-layer coaching stack:** Ria (AI), plus 600+ certified human coaches, plus a growing clinical layer for chronic and metabolic disease.
- **Signals and devices:** nutrition, steps, sleep, heart rate and CGM glucose. Device sales were ₹18.6 Cr in FY25.
- **GLP-1 programme:** coaching for users on weight-loss drugs. By Dec 2025 it was the fastest-growing offering, with a double-digit share of revenue. Management expects it to become more than one-third of paid subscriptions.

**Takeaway:** the company moved to near break-even mainly by cutting costs (people and ads), not by growing. Revenue has fallen for two straight years. Some secondary blogs say Healthify was "profitable in FY24". That conflicts with the reported ₹88 Cr FY24 loss.

## Strategic moves
1. **Dec 2025, Novo Nordisk India:** Healthify's first drugmaker partnership, a patient-support/coaching programme for people prescribed Novo's weight-loss drugs. Healthify said it was seeking more pharma deals.
2. **US push:** fresh capital was reported for US expansion (figures in coverage range from $20M to $45M).
3. **Aug 2026, merger with Berry Street (US):**
   - Berry Street brings 2,000+ registered dietitians/clinicians and insurance-covered nutrition and GLP-1 care.
   - Healthify brings Ria and its consumer scale.
   - Co-CEOs are Noah Kotlove (Berry Street) and Tushar Vashisht.
   - The combined company uses the Berry Street brand in the US and the Healthify brand in India and international markets.
   - Terms were undisclosed (one secondary source says all-stock).
   - The companies pitch the combination as the largest insurance-covered AI metabolic-health platform in the US.

## Competitive landscape
- **India:** Fittr (coach network and community), Cult.fit (workouts and gyms), plus a growing number of AI calorie-tracker apps.
- **Global and US:** MyFitnessPal (food database), Noom (behavioural approach), Omada, Hims & Hers, and other GLP-1 care platforms.
- **Healthify's edge:** a food database localised for Indian and other cuisines, a hybrid AI-plus-human coaching model, and (after the merger) US insurance reimbursement.

## Open questions and risks
- **Revenue decline:** can GLP-1 and US insurance revenue reverse the FY24–FY25 drop?
- **Pharma dependence:** GLP-1 coaching relies on drugmaker partnerships and on drug pricing and access (e.g., generic semaglutide in India after its 2026 patent expiry).
- **Integration:** combining the two companies across India and the US under two brands and two CEOs.
- **Missing financials:** consolidated figures (including US and export entities) and the post-merger valuation have not been disclosed.
